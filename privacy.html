Privacy

DIO Deals is a deal discovery site. It does not require shopper accounts, and this version does not ask for names, email addresses, passwords, payment details, or precise location.

Saved offers and the local click counter are stored in your browser using local storage. They are not shared between devices. Clearing browser data removes them.

Offer listings are fetched from DealBuffalo's public JSON API. When you open an offer, the destination may pass through the source publisher's click-tracking link before redirecting to the retailer. The source publisher and retailer have their own privacy practices.

This early version does not run shared DIO analytics or a DIO affiliate conversion system. Future changes to data collection will be reflected here.

Prices and availability can change; verify the final details with the retailer.
